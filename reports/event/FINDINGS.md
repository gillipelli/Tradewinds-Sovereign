# 2026–2027 event assessment

As of 2026-09-07, using NOAA's 2026-08-13 forecast.

The modeled cumulative revenue loss excluding Australia is $0.68bn (90% range $-2.21bn to $4.37bn); P(loss)=63.3%. Positive-shortfall ES95 is $6.21bn.

These are event-specific conditional risk estimates, not observed losses or a validated official budget forecast. The fiscal model estimates historical associations; it does not identify a causal tax multiplier. Neutral and event cases share non-climate shocks. Negative losses represent gains.

These estimates apply to the agriculture-linked revenue channel in constant 2025 USD. They exclude inflation/FX feedback, emergency expenditure, and broader economic spillovers. The late-2027 climate extension is model-generated.
